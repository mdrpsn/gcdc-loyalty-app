Got Cha Dink Club App: how a session runs

From adding a venue to giving a reward. Website: playpass.gotchadinkclub.com

The one idea behind everything: one OpenPlay attended = one point toward rewards. The app counts OpenPlays attended, not games or matches played. A player's attendance only counts after an admin approves it, and rewards are only given after the session has been marked Complete.

0Logging in

  • Log in at /login/ on your phone or laptop. Your own email and your own password. Never share your password.
  • The main screen is the Organizer desk. Its menu has: OpenPlays, Players, Attendance approvals, Reward requests, Link accounts, Audit.
  • Forgot your password? On the login page tap Forgot password and use the link that arrives by email.

1Add a venue (once per place)

Admin / Organizer
  1. On the Organizer desk tap Add a venue.
  2. Type the venue name and save. Check the spelling first. There is no rename or delete screen, so a wrong name has to be fixed by whoever manages the website.

2Schedule an OpenPlay

Admin / Organizer
  1. On the Organizer desk tap Schedule an OpenPlay.
  2. Choose the venue.
  3. Type a Session name if you want one, for example Sunday Morning OpenPlay (up to 80 characters). Players see this name. It is optional: if you leave it blank, the session is called "OpenPlay at" plus the venue.
  4. Choose Starts and Ends. Times are Philippine time.
  5. Save, then look at the session card to make sure the name, date and times are right.

Wrong name? Open the session and tap Rename OpenPlay. Type the new name and tap Save name. Leave it blank to go back to "OpenPlay at" plus the venue. You can rename a session at any time, even after it is finished, and every rename is recorded in the audit log.

Wrong date, time or venue? Those cannot be edited. Open the session, cancel it with a reason, and schedule it again. Nothing is ever deleted. The cancelled one stays in the history.

3Players sign up (once per player)

Player
  1. The player opens /signup/ and enters their email. A link arrives by email and works for 30 minutes.
  2. After tapping the link they fill in: full name, contact number, ReClub name, ReClub profile link, and a password.
  3. They can now register for sessions and submit attendance.

What is "Link accounts" for? It joins a person's new login to an older record the club already has for them (for example someone we wrote down before the app existed), so their past attendance is not lost.

  • Only an admin can do it, and only for someone the club already had on record. A brand-new player never needs it.
  • If a player's ReClub link is already in our records, sign-up is blocked on purpose. That is the signal to use Link accounts.
  • How: Link accounts → type the account's exact email → compare the old and new records side by side → tick "I verified this person's identity outside the app" → confirm.
  • Check who they are first (for example their ReClub profile and past sessions). Never link just because the emails look alike. There is no undo and no "merge players" button.
  • An old attendance entry that was never linked cannot be approved until its player is linked to an account.

4Players register for a session

Player
  1. Open Upcoming OpenPlays and tap Register next to the session.
  2. Changed their mind? Tap Cancel registration. This only works before the session starts.

5On the day: attendance

Admin / Organizer Player
  1. Open the session on your phone to see its roster (registered players). Players find the session by its name, date, time and venue, so a clear name helps. If a name is wrong, tap Rename OpenPlay on the session page.
  2. After the session has started, attendance can be sent for approval in either of two ways:
    • The player does it: Submit attendance → pick the OpenPlay → Submit for approval.
    • Staff does it for a signed-up player from the roster. It is the same submission.
  3. A submission shows as Pending approval. It does not count yet.

Players who have not signed up cannot be added on the spot. They must sign up first (step 3). The old "walk-in with no account" shortcut is switched off on purpose.

6Approve attendance

Admin only
  1. Open Attendance approvals. Use the tabs: All / Pending approval / Approved / Declined.
  2. For each submission compare the session, the roster, the player's details, and their ReClub profile.
  3. Tap Approve, or Decline and type a reason. A declined player can send it again.

Never approve someone who was not there. Approving is what gives the player their point toward rewards.

7Complete the session

Admin / Organizer
  1. Only after the scheduled end time, open the session and tap Review and complete OpenPlay.
  2. Check the two numbers: Checked in and Registered, unchecked. Unchecked players are not counted as present.
  3. Tap Confirm completion.

Don't forget this step. If a session is never completed, players' progress shows but reward eligibility stays pending. Never issue a reward by hand. Complete the session and the app does it.

How a player uses a discount or free OpenPlay

Player
  1. My rewards → Use reward → choose the upcoming OpenPlay they want to use it for → Request this reward.
  2. Sending the request does not use up the reward, and they can cancel it until it is approved.
  3. Discount: they pay the regular fee less ₱50 by GCash or cash and send proof of payment to the club as usual. Free OpenPlay: no payment for that session.

How you approve it

Admin only
  1. Open Reward requests → Requested.
  2. The card tells you what to collect (the fee less ₱50, or nothing for a free OpenPlay). Check the payment first (GCash or cash).
  3. Tap Approve and redeem. Or Decline with a reason, or Release to free the reward again.

Giving a reward directly (overgrip, shirt, or at the court)

Admin / Organizer
  1. Open Players, find the player, check the reward is Available.
  2. Tap Redeem, choose the OpenPlay if it applies, tap Confirm redemption.
  3. Hand over the benefit yourself. The app records the use only. It does not take payment.

Expired rewards

Admin only
  • An expired reward cannot be used normally. An admin can Extend expiry (with a reason), or redeem it as a goodwill exception with a reason. Both are recorded in the audit log.

9Corrections (admin only)

  • Wrong attendance: open the player, tap Correct next to that attendance, and give a specific reason. The record is marked void but stays in the history, and an admin can restore it later.
  • Forgot someone after the session is done: on a completed session an admin uses Add historical attendance, searches the player, and gives a reason.
  • A completed session was a mistake: only an admin can cancel it (Cancel OpenPlay), with a reason. Attendance and rewards are recalculated, nothing is deleted.
  • Who did what? Audit shows every action, newest first. It is read-only. Don't type passwords into any reason box.

✓Quick checklist for every session

  1. Venue exists and the session is scheduled with the right date and times.
  2. Players are signed up and registered.
  3. After the start: attendance submitted.
  4. Admin approves only the people who were actually there.
  5. After the scheduled end: complete the session (Review and complete OpenPlay → Confirm completion).
  6. Rewards: check Reward requests, confirm payment, approve.

House rules: keep your password private and unique. Players' names, numbers and ReClub links are personal data, so use them only for running sessions. Don't create fake players or fake sessions on the real site. Money (GCash and cash) is handled outside the app.
