How PlayPass works

One OpenPlay attended counts as one point. No scorekeeping, no extra apps to learn.

  1. Sign up

    Create your PlayPass with your email. We send you a link to confirm it.

  2. Come play

    Join an OpenPlay. After the session, your attendance is confirmed by the club.

  3. Earn rewards

    Hit a milestone and your reward appears in your account, ready to use.

Keep showing up. We reward it.
